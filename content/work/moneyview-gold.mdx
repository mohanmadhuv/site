A Moneyview user opens the app to check on their loan. A banner says invest in digital gold. They close it — not because they don't want to invest, but because nothing in that moment explained why this, or why here, from an app they came to for borrowing.

<Media />

Gold at Moneyview was built to answer that question before it got asked. Context-first onboarding, a jewellery store partnership for physical redemption, and default amounts matched to first-time investors — every detail traced back to the same gap research surfaced: users trusted physical gold, not digital promises.

<Media />

## Why it worked

Most digital gold apps compete on rates and polish. Moneyview's users weren't comparing products — most had never bought digital gold at all.

Designing for a first investment, not a product switch, changed the framing entirely. Trust signals stacked early. Amounts started small. Post-purchase flows were built for return visits, not exit screens.

50,000+ transactions in three months. Repeat purchases up 36%. One of Moneyview's best-performing verticals after lending.
