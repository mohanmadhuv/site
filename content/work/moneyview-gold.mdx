When Moneyview users open the app to take a loan, and a banner says start an investment, they close it. Not because they don't want to invest, but because nothing in that moment explained why is coming from an app they came to for borrowing money.

<Media />

Digital Gold at Moneyview was built to answer that question. Context-first onboarding, a jewellery store partnership for physical redemption, and default amounts matched to first-time investors. Every detail traced back to the same gap that customer interviews surfaced: users trusted physical gold, not digital promises.

<Media />

## Why it worked

Most digital gold apps compete on rates and sales. Moneyview's users weren't even comparing products because most had never bought digital gold at all.

Designing for a first investment, and not a product, changed the framing entirely. Amounts started small, but they started snowballing.

50,000+ transactions in three months. Repeat purchases up 36%. One of Moneyview's best-performing verticals after lending.
