A preceptor supervising a half-day clinic gives verbal feedback to residents after every patient, then has to write it all down separately. Under time pressure, that second step gets skipped, and the resident loses feedback.

<Media />

FieldQuote doesn't add a step. The preceptor picks the resident, speaks the debrief by dictation instead of writing, and done.

## Why it's different

Most of the 44 teams at the hackathon built another inbox to triage, another portal to log into, another subscription and the learning curve for the tool add ons stacked on top of an already overloaded clinic.

FieldQuote doesn't compete on doing more, it fits inside a step that already exists, so nothing new has to be adopted.
